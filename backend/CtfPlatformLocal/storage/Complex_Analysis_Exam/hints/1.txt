Look carefully at the signature parameters. If two signatures share the same r, something critical may have been reused. 
Recall that for two signatures (r, s1), (r, s2) on different messages:
k = (z1 - z2)*(s1 - s2)^(-1) mod n
Once k is known, recover the private key d using equation s1 = (z1 + r*d)*k^(-1) mod n
