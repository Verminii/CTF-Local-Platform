Professor's Notes
=================

Candidates must solve at least one of the problems in the coded examination.
Limit your work to clear and rigorous arguments; you may consult mathematical forums, books, articles, and other external references.
Each problem originates from mathematical discussion archives.
Only complete solutions will be considered for evaluation. Submissions consisting only of final results, without justification, will receive 0 points.

All problems were collected from various mathematical forum discussions.
Some original threads may no longer be active or may have been marked as [closed].

Administrative note:
You have 8 hours to solve one of the four problems.

Archival note:
Reference solutions were stored separately.
One participant is known to have solved all four unusually quickly; part of the task is to determine their identity and to verify your own solutions accordingly.
